# Sound credits

The soundboard's cat sounds are clips of recordings from Wikimedia Commons: each was trimmed to its
best one to three seconds, faded in and out, peak-normalized and converted to a 64 kbit/s mono MP3.
The clips keep the license of the recording they come from.
